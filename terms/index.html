Memory Stroll Pro

Memory Stroll Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, billed weekly or yearly at the price shown in the app before you buy. The weekly plan may start with a free trial; the trial length and the date of the first charge are shown under the purchase button. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or when the trial ends.

The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in the iPhone Settings app, under your name, then Subscriptions.

What the free version includes

One palace with up to 12 spots and up to 2 plain lists, with spaced review and the daily drill. Pro adds unlimited palaces, spots and lists, talks and speeches, names, long numbers and wrist drills on Apple Watch.
