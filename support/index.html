Lanternwalk support

Building a palace

Pick a place you know by heart. Name its spots in the order you walk past them. Order matters: each item is placed on the next spot along the route.

Placing a list

Open Lists, tap +, type one item per line and pick a palace. Lanternwalk suggests a vivid picture for each spot; change it to make it yours.

Apple Watch

Open Lanternwalk on your iPhone once with the Watch nearby. The Watch then shows your next spots. Tap Reveal, then Got it or Missed; the result updates the review schedule on your iPhone. Wrist drills are part of Lanternwalk Pro.

Subscriptions

Lanternwalk Pro renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel in the iPhone Settings app, under your name, then Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before renewal. To restore a purchase, open Settings in Lanternwalk and tap Restore purchases.

Deleting your data

Settings, then Erase all palaces and lists. Everything is stored only on your iPhone.
