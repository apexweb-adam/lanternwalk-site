Memory Stroll

Memory Stroll teaches the memory palace on iPhone and Apple Watch. You place each thing you want to remember on a spot in a place you already know, such as your home or your daily route, then walk it back in your mind.

Build palaces room by room, place lists, talks, names and long numbers on their spots, and review them on a spaced schedule. Wrist drills on Apple Watch send each result back to your iPhone.
