Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026.

Memory Stroll is made by Adam Tokar, sole trader, Hungary ("I"). This policy covers the Memory Stroll app for iPhone and Apple Watch.

What the app stores

Your palaces, rooms, spots, lists, pictures, review schedule and streak are stored only on your iPhone. The Apple Watch app receives the next spots to check from your iPhone over Apple's Watch connection and sends your results back. Nothing is sent to me or to any server I run.

What I collect

Nothing. Memory Stroll has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It contains no third-party SDKs and uses no AI service.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. I do not see your name, email or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the trial reminder and the daily drill reminder are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server.

Deleting your data

Open Memory Stroll, tap Settings, then Erase all palaces and lists. Deleting the app also deletes everything it stored.

Children

Memory Stroll is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.
